## definition

digital garden (*plural* digital gardens)

1. A public, evolving online space for cultivating ideas, notes, and knowledge, unlike a traditional [blog](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/blog#English "blog")'s finished posts; where thoughts grow organically, are continuously edited (like [pruning](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/pruning#English "pruning")), linked together [non-linearly](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/non-linear#English "non-linear"), and shared in various stages from "[seedlings](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/seedlings#English "seedlings")" (raw ideas) to "[evergreens](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/evergreens#English "evergreens")" (mature concepts).
